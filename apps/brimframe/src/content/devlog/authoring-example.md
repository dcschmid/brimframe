This is an authoring example, not studio news. Keep it as a draft or delete it
before writing your first real update.

## Writing an update

Describe work that actually happened. Use short paragraphs, meaningful headings
and descriptive links. Do not repeat the page title as a Markdown heading.

Write from Daniel's solo-development perspective. First person is welcome:
describe what you worked on, the choices you made and what you learned. Do not
turn personal notes into a corporate plural voice or invent development history.

For an optional cover, add `heroImage: './images/your-image.png'` and a descriptive
`heroImageAlt` in the frontmatter. Use artwork you own and keep its source file.

Set `draft: false` only when the content is ready to publish. Drafts never receive
a public article URL, even in the development server.
