I’m working towards the first public demo of Riff Racers, aiming for December 2026. There’s still work to do before I can put a build out, particularly on the
racing AI and handling.

## What I’m aiming for

The demo plan currently includes:

- Five tracks
- 12 drivers and 12 karts
- Multiple difficulty levels
- Windows and Linux builds

That’s a lot to finish on my own. I want enough variety for people to get a
feel for the game, but I may need to adjust the scope as I go.

## The five tracks

Each track is meant to have its own layout and atmosphere. I’m trying out
shortcuts, risky jumps, alternative routes and pit lanes, then seeing how they
affect a race. Some of those ideas will get cut if they don’t work well in play.

## Twelve drivers, twelve karts

I want you to be able to tell the drivers and karts apart by their designs,
even before you look at the colours. I’m still working on the models and
animations, and there’s balancing to do as well.

## The racing still needs work too

Most of my time goes into the racing itself: handling, the way races play out,
pit stops and whether you can see what’s happening at speed. I’m also working
on performance across different hardware.

The computer-controlled drivers need particular attention. At higher
difficulties, I want them to choose better racing lines, use shortcuts and be
more aggressive. Simply giving them more speed wouldn’t do that. Getting
those decisions right is still taking work.

## The look of the game

I’m still revising the visual style and going back over some tracks several
times. Screenshots show the build they were taken from; parts of a track may
look different by the time you play the demo.

## Why release a demo?

I need to find out how the game feels to people who haven’t been building it.
Can you spot the shortcuts while racing? Does the difficulty feel fair? Where
does a race become frustrating?

I’d also like to hear what people think of the drivers and karts. Releasing a
demo gives me a chance to act on that feedback while I’m still developing the
game.

## When you can try it

December 2026 is still a target rather than a confirmed release date. If the
racing needs more time, I’ll move the demo and post the update here.

— Daniel
